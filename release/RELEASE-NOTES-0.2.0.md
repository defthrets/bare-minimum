Hunger, thirst and sleep on the game's own clock, with health, armour and energy standing in the same row. Six bars beside the minimap, a frame round it, and the street you are on written above it.

## What is new in 0.2.0

**A third need: thirst.** Twenty-four game hours from full to dry — well under half of hunger's, so it empties about as often as you sleep — and one can fixes it where a day's food is several meals. That ratio is the point: a meter running on hunger's clock would be a second hunger bar you fix at the same counter.

What fills it is worked out rather than typed. A drink is most of the bar, food carries across a fifth of its own hunger value, and **alcohol subtracts on a curve** — so a beer is a drink and a whisky is very much not.

Being dry does not slow you down. It takes your **wind**: the sprint meter empties up to two and a half times faster, on a bar already in the row, so the cause and the effect sit two columns apart. Then he starts breathing hard in his own voice, and the rate of it is the reading.

**The drugs cost something.** A stimulant used to pin the energy bar and then simply let go; now there is a comedown. Two bars of a sedative and the screen starts going out for a second at a time — no warning, and no loss of control, because where you have got to by the time it comes back is the mechanic. LSD gets twelve minutes of the game's own peyote timecycle, swelling on two periods that do not divide into each other so it never settles into a filter and never falls into a rhythm.

**Health is red the whole way**, vivid at full and dark at nothing, so every bar reads by one rule: brightness says how bad, hue says which meter. The low-health beat goes black rather than white — on a red bar, going pale reads as the level *rising* — which leaves white free to mean one thing, and it means it the instant you are hit.

**Every vending machine you walk past stays on the map**, remembered by position and kept between sessions. There is a survey on the menu that will fly the map and find the lot, and it is on Scroll Lock if you want it without opening anything.

**The vitals moved in.** Health, armour and energy were a separate mod; they stand in one row of six with hunger, thirst and sleep now, drawn through the same code with the same surround, channel, plate and mark.

## Which zip

- **`BareMinimum-0.2.0.zip`** — the mod on its own. Take this one if you already run other SHVDN mods.
- **`BareMinimum-0.2.0-full.zip`** — the same, with ScriptHookVDotNet bundled alongside it. Take this one if this is your first script mod.

Neither contains **ScriptHookV**, which is Alexander Blade's and has to come from his site.

## Installing

Unzip and drop the contents into your GTA V folder — the `scripts` folder merges with the one already there.

**Unblock the ZIP before you extract it**, or ScriptHookVDotNet refuses to load the DLL and says nothing about it.

No asset replacement, no `.rpf` edits. Legacy and Enhanced from one build.

Everything is in `BareMinimum.ini` — every threshold, rate, colour and position — and most of it is also on the in-game menu (**F11**), live. Your pocket is **F12**, and **E** is everything else.
