The bodies move in, a real bag, and a bench to sleep on.

**The dead are this mod's now.** Searching a body used to live in Posted Up and carrying one in Five0 Patrol, so the food on a corpse was only there if you ran the gang mod. Both are here. Stand over a body and hold **E** to search it — cash, food and drink, and drugs too if Posted Up 0.9.9 or newer is installed — then hold **E** again to pick it up and carry it. Guns fall to the ground when somebody dies, the way the game always did. Settings on the F11 menu under BODIES. Asked for by NordCyborg.

> **Running Five0 Patrol?** Its current download (0.2.1) still has a body carry of its own; its next one will not. Until then, turn one off — `[Bodies] Carry = false` in this mod's ini.

**A real bag on his back.** Posted Up's bag was a strap across the chest: one drawable, the same for every bag, and missing on half the tops in the game. It is a real bag model now — fifteen of the game's own, picked on the F11 menu under BAG, the tactical pack by default. Each has its own place on his back, and the position tool on the same page moves it a centimetre at a time on the numpad and saves it.

**The bag you put down is the bag you chose**, not a duffel, and each one has its own place on the ground, set with the same tool while it lies there. Needs Posted Up 0.9.9 or newer.

**Sleep on anything you could sleep on.** Fifteen of the nineteen bed names this mod looked for did not exist in the game, so beds were only ever found by four models — Michael's, Jimmy's, Trevor's and Lester's — and Franklin's two never worked. Every name now comes from the game's own object list:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| Beds | 56 | a full night, 8 hours, and a little health back |
| Couches | 112 | sofas, couches, loungers — 6 hours, four fifths of the rest |
| Rough | 72 | park and bus-stop benches, tents, homeless shelters, mattresses and sleeping bags on the ground — 5 hours |

A couch or a bench offers once you stop at it, not as you walk past. A bed from a housing or interior mod that is still not on the list goes in `[Sleeping] ExtraModels`. Reported by Vitalezzzz (M8T's Home Ownership houses), asked for by Jcats. How good each kind is, and whether couches and rough sleeping are on at all, is on the F11 menu under SLEEP.

**Franklin's fridge on Forum Drive.** The game's own "drink beer" is bolted to that fridge and always got the button first. A fridge of this mod's own stands in the same spot now, and only offers when you are right at it. Found by D_Cypher003, reported by NordCyborg.

**The food stays where it was fitted.** Everything sat right in his hand while being eaten and wrong the moment his arm came down — between bites, and in the pocket while you chose it, where it was in the wrong hand as well. The animation moves the bone the food hangs from; the mod measures by how much and holds the food off his wrist in the same place relative to his palm. The fitting bench can still set an arm-down position for any one item by hand. Asked for by GTA5gamers.

**Three bites, and the empty drops from his side.** A meal or a drink is three passes of the animation with a pause between each, then his arm comes down and the can or wrapper leaves his hand there, not at his mouth. Drinks were cut short by their own timer and never got the third sip; they do now.

**Smoking.** The animation stops when the cigarette does. The lit end smoulders the whole time it is in his hand, and he rests between drags instead of taking them back to back (`[Eating] SmokeBreakSeconds`, 3 by default).

**Your ini is not replaced.** Everything new takes its default until you copy its lines over from the ini in this download.
