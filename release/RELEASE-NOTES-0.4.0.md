Hunger, thirst and sleep on the game's own clock, with health, armour and energy standing in the same row. Six bars beside the minimap, a frame round it, and the street you are on written above it.

## What is new in 0.4.0

**157 of the 201 things you can buy have a picture of their own**, and so do all nine drugs. Pixel art, in colour, drawn per item rather than per kind: the Bleeder Burger drips, the Bacon Triple Cheese Melt has the bacon, the Money Shot has the egg, the Tacos al Pastor have the pineapple, the Blue Plate has the blue rim and the Sludgie is the blue slush cup. Every tile in the shop, the pocket and the fridge, and the toast when you eat.

**The white shapes are still there, underneath.** An item's own picture wins over its group's; anything not drawn yet shows the group's white shape, tinted as it always was, so a half-drawn set is a working mod and not a broken one. Drop an `i_<id>.png` into the icons folder and that item has a picture — no rebuild, no field to edit. The mod looks at the file to decide whether it is coloured art or a white shape.

**`[Minimap] X` and `Y`**, for the HUD-mover mods on ultrawide screens. They drag the radar to the corner, the game still reports its original position, and this mod built its frame round an empty rectangle in the middle of the screen. Two live rows under Vitals — Map left/right, Map up/down — move the frame, the bars, the strip and the fuel gauge to wherever your map actually is. Asked for on the mod page.

**Cutscenes hide the HUD by name.** It hid before only because the game hides its own HUD for them; it is a rule of its own now, gone the instant a cutscene starts. Also asked for.

## Which zip

- **`BareMinimum-0.4.0.zip`** — the mod on its own. Take this one if you already run other SHVDN mods.
- **`BareMinimum-0.4.0-full.zip`** — the same, with ScriptHookVDotNet bundled alongside it. Take this one if this is your first script mod.

Neither contains **ScriptHookV**, which is Alexander Blade's and has to come from his site.

## Installing

Unzip and drop the contents into your GTA V folder — the `scripts` folder merges with the one already there.

**Unblock the ZIP before you extract it**, or ScriptHookVDotNet refuses to load the DLL and says nothing about it.

No asset replacement, no `.rpf` edits. Legacy and Enhanced from one build.

Everything is in `BareMinimum.ini`, and most of it is also on the in-game menu (**F11**), live. Your pocket is **F12**, and **E** is everything else.
