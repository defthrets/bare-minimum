Missions keep their special ability, and the map keeps its missions.

**"Mr. Philips" can be finished.** The mission that teaches Trevor's special ability turns it on and waits for it to run out the game's own way before it clears the red screen. This mod runs the special ability off its energy bar, and to do that it kept the game's meter topped up — so the ability never ran out the game's way, the screen effect stayed, and the mission could not go on with the mod installed. While any mission is running now, the special ability is the game's: nothing tops it up, cuts it off or switches it off, it does not drink from the energy bar, and the game's own ability bar is shown again until the mission ends. Reported by D_Cypher003.

**Mission and shop markers stop disappearing.** Every vending machine and fruit stall you have ever passed kept a marker on the map — several hundred over a playthrough, on top of the shops — and the game can only hold so many blips. Once it is full, the story's own scripts cannot make theirs, and the mission letters and shop markers are what go missing. Now only the nearest **150** machines carry a marker, and they follow you round the map. Every machine is still remembered. `[Counters] MachineMarkersMax` sets the number; `0` marks them all, as before. Reported by Manfreedy.

**No sleeping on park benches or couches.** 0.9.0 added both and they are taken back out: sleeping rough is tents, homeless shelters, and mattresses and sleeping bags on the ground. Beds are unchanged.

**The log says more**, so the next report of either of the above comes with numbers: how many blips are on the map and how many are machine markers, when the minimap stops drawing, and how much memory the game is holding.

**Your ini is not replaced.** `MachineMarkersMax` takes its default until you copy its line over from the ini in this download.
