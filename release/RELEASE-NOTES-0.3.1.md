A point release over 0.3.0, and only the Hindi changed.

## What is fixed

**Hindi is in Latin letters now**, and it is the one on the menu because it is the one that draws.

0.3.0 shipped it in Devanagari — every word correct — and it came out as a row of empty boxes. GTA V has never had a Hindi release, so there is no font in the game with those letters in it. Correct text nobody can read is the most useless kind of correct.

Latin goes through the same font as English and works on every install, and romanised Hindi is not a compromise invented for this: it is how a great deal of Hindi is actually typed. All 120 phrases, checked to hold nothing the game's font does not have.

The Devanagari is kept whole as `hi-Deva` and works the day somebody puts a font in the game that has it. Its menu row cannot be written in its own letters — that is the entire problem — so it is labelled in Latin and tells you why it did not draw if you pick it.

**Nothing else changed.** If you are running 0.3.0 and you do not read Hindi, this is not worth the download.

## Which zip

- **`BareMinimum-0.3.1.zip`** - the mod on its own. Take this one if you already run other SHVDN mods.
- **`BareMinimum-0.3.1-full.zip`** - the same, with ScriptHookVDotNet bundled alongside it. Take this one if this is your first script mod.

Neither contains **ScriptHookV**, which is Alexander Blade's and has to come from his site.

## Installing

Unzip and drop the contents into your GTA V folder - the `scripts` folder merges with the one already there.

**Unblock the ZIP before you extract it**, or ScriptHookVDotNet refuses to load the DLL and says nothing about it.

Everything is in `BareMinimum.ini`, and most of it is also on the in-game menu (**F11**), live. Your pocket is **F12**, and **E** is everything else.
