--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. ONE PARAGRAPH  (short description)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hunger and sleep for GTA V, as two thin gauges low on the screen with a chicken
drumstick and a moon underneath them. They do not just drain -- hunger churns,
and gets more agitated the emptier it is; sleep breathes, and the breath slows
as you tire. There are 108 places to eat and they are the real ones: the taco
windows, the hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Bean Machine, Rob's Liquor, the delis
and the restaurants nobody ever gets to walk into. 157 things to buy, each with
its own price, its own effect and its own line about itself. Shops with a full
menu, stalls that just hand you a hot dog, and drive-throughs you order from
without leaving the car. What you buy goes in your pocket for later, or into the
fridge in any safehouse kitchen. Sleep in any bed. Run everywhere and you will
pay for it in both. 97 settings, all live in an in-game menu, and every shop and
every item is a plain text file you can edit. Drop-in install, works on Legacy
and Enhanced, and your save is never touched. A work in progress: the shop list
is still growing, and adding your own is a text file.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. FULL DESCRIPTION  (main body)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🍗 BARE MINIMUM 🌙

Franklin gets hungry and he gets tired. Two thin gauges sit low on the screen
and quietly empty until you do something about it.


📊 TWO GAUGES THAT ACTUALLY MOVE

Hunger on the left, sleep on the right, standing on the bottom edge out past
the minimap. A chicken drumstick and a moon underneath say which is which.

They are animated, and they move DIFFERENTLY on purpose:

  🍗  HUNGER CHURNS.  An unsettled surface with the contents turning over
      underneath, and it gets more agitated the emptier it is -- which is the
      opposite of what a fuel gauge does.

  🌙  SLEEP BREATHES.  The whole gauge rises and falls as one, and the breath
      slows and deepens the more tired you are.

The colour carries the state as much as the level does. Hunger runs from a
vivid green down through olive to a dead brown; sleep from an awake blue down
through violet to a deep, sunk purple. When either is nearly out it sways, and
you catch it out of the corner of your eye without looking for it.

There is a second HUD in the box. Switch HUD style to ICONS and the bars come
off entirely, leaving the drumstick and the moon on their own, still carrying
the same colour. It tells you less on purpose. One press in the menu, either
way.

Both run on the game clock, so a full day of them is about 48 minutes of play,
and both pause when the game does.


🌮 MORE THAN A HUNDRED PLACES TO EAT

108 of them, and they are the shops that are already there: the taco windows,
the hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Taco Bomb, Bean Machine, Cool Beans,
Rusty Brown's, Pizza This, Rob's Liquor, thirteen liquor stores, the corner
stores, the delis, and a good few restaurants the game never lets you into. Del
Perro and Vespucci are covered end to end.

Each has its own menu, its own prices, its own hours and its own voice. A
breakfast menu at midnight is shown greyed with its hours on it rather than
quietly missing, so you learn to come back.

157 things to buy -- 86 meals, 36 snacks and 35 drinks. Drink enough of the
wrong ones and you will feel it, and the walk goes with it.

🚧 STILL GROWING, AND THE THIN PART IS NAMED so you know what you are getting:
the convenience stores and the fuel stations. A handful are in and most are
not, and they are the next thing going in. Everything that IS in is finished --
menu, prices, hours, the lot -- and vendors.json is plain text, so a door I
have not got to yet is a coordinate and a name away from working.


🛒 HOW YOU BUY IT

Walk up and press E. A counter or a shelf opens a list with tabs, a picture and
a price on every row, and a line about each one.

  🌭  A hot dog cart just hands you a hot dog, and he eats it on the spot.
  🚗  Drive-throughs work from the driver's seat. A meal at the wheel is eaten
      slowly between junctions instead of in four seconds at the window, and
      the horn is held off while you are ordering.
  🎒  What you buy goes in your pocket by default -- five things, on F11 -- so
      a shop is somewhere you go back to rather than a place you stand and eat.
  🧊  Every safehouse kitchen has a working fridge that holds forty things.
      Your pocket on one side, the fridge on the other, one key to move things
      across and another to eat from either side. It is found by looking for a
      fridge rather than by a coordinate, so interior mods work too.


⚠️ IT COSTS SOMETHING TO IGNORE

Sprinting empties both faster than walking, and the gauges visibly speed up
while you do it, so the reason is on screen.

  🍗  Let hunger reach nothing and it starts costing health, slowly, with a
      warning first. It stops well above the floor -- it will never kill you.
  🌙  Let sleep reach nothing and the picture starts to swim. Stay up through
      that and you collapse where you stand and wake up four hours later.

Sleep in any bed, and how well you sleep depends on whose bed it is. A car is
worse than a safehouse and better than nothing. Sleeping also sobers you up,
because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.


🔧 YOURS TO CHANGE

97 settings across six tabs in an in-game menu on F7, each with a line saying
what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes written back to
the ini without disturbing your comments or your ordering.

Every shop and every item is a plain text file. Add a shop, change a price,
write a menu, move something that is in the wrong place. Anything the game
cannot find is skipped with a line in the log rather than breaking the mod.


📱 IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH

The shops talk on its social feed -- 71 accounts and 475 lines, written as
people who sell lunch rather than as gang members. Entirely optional, entirely
one-way, and completely silent if you do not have it.


💾 INSTALL

Drag into your GTA V folder, say yes to merging, load a save. Needs ScriptHookV
and ScriptHookVDotNet 3. Works on Legacy and Enhanced from the same files. No
game file is modified and nothing is written to your save -- delete three
things and it never happened.

❗ UNBLOCK THE ZIP BEFORE YOU EXTRACT IT, or ScriptHookVDotNet will refuse to
load the DLL and say nothing about it.


⌨️ KEYS

  E     interact -- the counter, the bed and the car alike
  F7    settings
  F11   your pocket

All three can be changed in the ini.

  4. CHANGELOG  (for the mod page's changelog field)

0.1.1

  🍗 The bars are the default HUD now. The drumstick-and-moon icons are still in
  the box and still one press away in the F7 menu, they are just no longer what
  you get without asking for it.

  ⚙️ Ships set up the way it is actually played: the gauges' size and animation,
  the pocket at five slots, the hunger and sleep clocks, and shop markers on the
  pause map.

  🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Michael, Franklin and Trevor were labelled wrong internally and had been
  since the first build -- the model hashes were a person out of step. Nothing
  was shared or lost between characters, but the names in the log and in the
  save files were wrong. Your save is corrected once, automatically, the first
  time 0.1.1 runs.


0.1.0

  First release.

  Hunger and sleep on the game clock, with a bar HUD and an icon HUD. 108 shops,
  stalls, windows and drive-throughs. 157 items. A pocket, a fridge in every
  safehouse kitchen, and sleeping in any bed.

  Sprinting costs you both. Hunger at zero takes health slowly and will not kill
  you. Sleep at zero swims the picture and then puts you on the floor.

  97 settings in an in-game menu. Every shop and every item is editable text.

  Optional one-way integration with Hoodrich's social feed.

  A work in progress. Not every food location is in yet -- the convenience
  stores and the fuel stations especially -- and more go in with each update.
