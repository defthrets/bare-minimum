  1. ONE LINE  (summary field)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Franklin gets hungry and he gets tired. More than a hundred real shops to do
something about it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2. ONE PARAGRAPH  (short description)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hunger and sleep for GTA V, as two small marks beside the minimap rather than
two more bars -- an apple eaten down to a core, an eye slowly closing, so you
read the state from the shape at a glance. There are 108 places to eat and they
are the real ones: the 24/7 you walk past every mission, the taco windows, the
hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Bean Machine, Rob's Liquor, the delis and the
restaurants nobody ever gets to walk into. 157 things to buy, each with its own
price, its own effect and its own line about itself. Shops with a full menu,
stalls that just hand you a hot dog, and drive-throughs you order from without
leaving the car. What you buy goes in your pocket for later, or into the fridge
in any safehouse kitchen. Sleep in any bed. Run everywhere and you will pay for
it in both. Ninety-seven settings, all live in an in-game menu, and every shop
and every item is a plain text file you can edit. Drop-in install, works on
Legacy and Enhanced, no OpenIV, and your save is never touched. A work in
progress: the shop list is still growing, and adding your own is a text file.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  3. FULL DESCRIPTION  (main body)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TWO MARKS, NOT TWO BARS

Hunger and sleep sit beside the minimap as an apple and a moon. Each goes
through five separate drawings as it empties -- the apple is eaten down to a
core, the eye closes -- so the state is carried by the shape rather than by the
length of a line. If you would rather have bars, there is a bar style in the
settings.

Both run on the game clock, so a full day of them is about 48 minutes of play.


MORE THAN A HUNDRED PLACES TO EAT

108 of them, and they are the shops that are already there: the taco windows,
the hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Taco Bomb, Bean Machine, Cool Beans,
Rusty Brown's, Pizza This, Rob's Liquor, thirteen liquor stores, the corner
stores, the delis, and a good few restaurants the game never lets you into. Del
Perro and Vespucci are covered end to end.

STILL GROWING, AND THE THIN PART IS NAMED SO YOU KNOW WHAT YOU ARE GETTING: the
convenience stores and the fuel stations. A handful are in and most are not, and
they are the next thing going in. Everything that IS in is finished -- menu,
prices, hours, the lot -- and vendors.json is plain text, so a door I have not
got to yet is a coordinate and a name away from working.

Each has its own menu, its own prices, its own hours and its own voice. A
breakfast menu at midnight is shown greyed with its hours on it rather than
quietly missing, so you learn to come back.

157 things to buy across food, snacks and drinks. Drink enough of the wrong
ones and you will feel it, and the walk goes with it.


HOW YOU BUY IT

Walk up and press E. A counter or a shelf opens a list with tabs, a picture and
a price per row, and a line about each one. A hot dog cart just hands you a hot
dog and he eats it on the spot.

Drive-throughs work from the driver's seat. A meal at the wheel is eaten slowly
between junctions instead of in four seconds at the window, and the horn is held
off while you are ordering.

What you buy goes in your pocket by default, so a shop is somewhere you go back
to rather than a place you stand and eat. Every safehouse kitchen has a working
fridge that holds forty things -- your pocket on one side, the fridge on the
other, one key to move things across and another to eat from either side.


IT COSTS SOMETHING TO IGNORE

Sprinting empties both meters faster than walking, and the marks visibly speed
up while you do it, so the reason is on screen.

Let hunger reach nothing and it starts costing health, slowly, with a warning
first. It stops well above the floor -- it will never kill you.

Let sleep reach nothing and the picture starts to swim. Stay up through that
and you collapse where you stand and wake up four hours later.

Sleep in any bed, and how well you sleep depends on whose bed it is. Sleeping
also sobers you up, because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.


YOURS TO CHANGE

Ninety-seven settings across nine tabs in an in-game menu on F7, each with a
line saying what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes
written back to the ini without disturbing your comments or your ordering.

Every shop and every item is a plain text file. Add a shop, change a price,
write a menu, move something that is in the wrong place. Anything the game
cannot find is skipped with a line in the log rather than breaking the mod.


IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH

The shops talk on its social feed -- 71 accounts and 475 lines, written as
people who sell lunch rather than as gang members. Entirely optional, entirely
one-way, and completely silent if you do not have it.


INSTALL

Drag into your GTA V folder, say yes to merging, load a save. Needs
ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet 3. Works on Legacy and Enhanced from the same
files. No game file is modified and nothing is written to your save -- delete
three things and it never happened.

UNBLOCK THE ZIP BEFORE YOU EXTRACT IT, or ScriptHookVDotNet will refuse to load
the DLL and say nothing about it.
