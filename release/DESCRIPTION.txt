<b>🍗 BARE MINIMUM 🌙</b>

Franklin gets hungry, he gets thirsty and he gets tired. A row of thin gauges sits beside the minimap and quietly empties until you do something about it.

🇬🇧 🇺🇸 🇧🇷 🇪🇸 🇫🇷 🇩🇪 🇷🇺 🇵🇱 🇮🇳 🇨🇳 — English, Português (BR), Español, Français, Deutsch, Русский, Polski, Hindi, 中文. First row of the settings menu. Anything a translation has not covered shows in English rather than going blank.

Free. Open source under MIT. Nightly builds at spitmux.me if you want it before it lands here.


<b>📊 ONE ROW OF GAUGES THAT ACTUALLY MOVE</b>

Health, sleep, hunger, thirst and your sprint energy, standing in a row beside the minimap inside a frame that also carries the street you are on and the suburb you are in. A mark under each says which is which.

Every one is a liquid, and they move <i>differently</i> on purpose:

<ul>
<li>❤️ <b>Health has a heartbeat.</b> Cells drift in it and get shoved up the column on every beat, and the beat quickens when he runs and when he is hurt — you can take his pulse off the bar. It throbs dark as it goes, and flashes white the instant he is hit.</li>
<li>🍗 <b>Hunger churns.</b> An unsettled surface with the contents turning over underneath, and it gets more agitated the emptier it is — which is the opposite of what a fuel gauge does.</li>
<li>💧 <b>Thirst runs clean.</b> No sediment, bubbles going <i>up</i> rather than crumbs going down, and it calms as it empties, because a glass with an inch left does not slosh.</li>
<li>🌙 <b>Sleep breathes.</b> The whole gauge rises and falls as one, and the breath slows and deepens the more tired you are.</li>
<li>⚡ <b>Energy runs charge along it</b> in lanes, up while it rebuilds and down while you spend it.</li>
</ul>

The colour carries the state as much as the level does, and every bar follows one rule: <i>brightness says how bad, hue says which meter</i>. Health is red, hunger orange, thirst cyan, sleep purple, energy a fluorescent green-yellow — no two share a family, and all of them darken as they empty.

<b>Franklin's special ability runs off the energy bar.</b> Use it and you watch it go.

<b>Go under the water and an air meter takes the plate under the map</b>, drawn where the game's own used to be, and it beats faster the less you have left.

<b>There is a second HUD in the box.</b> Switch HUD style to ICONS and the bars come off entirely, leaving the marks on their own, still carrying the same colour. It tells you less on purpose. One press in the menu, either way.

All three needs run on the <i>game</i> clock, so a full day of them is about 48 minutes of play, and they pause when the game does.


<b>🌮 TWO HUNDRED PLACES TO EAT AND DRINK</b>

211 of them, and they are the shops that are already there: the taco windows, the hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Taco Bomb, Bean Machine, Cool Beans, Rusty Brown's, Pizza This, Rob's Liquor, thirteen liquor stores, the corner stores, the delis, and a good few restaurants the game never lets you into. Del Perro and Vespucci are covered end to end.

Each has its own menu, its own prices, its own hours and its own voice. A breakfast menu at midnight is shown greyed with its hours on it rather than quietly missing, so you learn to come back.

201 things to buy — 111 meals, 50 snacks and 40 drinks — and <b>most of them have a picture of their own</b>, pixel art drawn per item rather than per kind: the Bleeder Burger drips, the Bacon Triple Cheese Melt has the bacon, the Tacos al Pastor have the pineapple, the Blue Plate has the blue rim. Drink enough of the wrong ones and you will feel it, and the walk goes with it.

<b>🥤 Every vending machine and fruit stall you walk past goes on the map for good.</b> They are found by model rather than from a list, so every one in the world works — and they are remembered by where they stand, so the map fills itself in as you play and stays filled in between sessions. There is a survey on the menu that will fly the map and find the lot for you if you would rather not wait.

<b>🚧 Still growing, and the thin part is named so you know what you are getting:</b> the convenience stores and the fuel stations. Everything that <i>is</i> in is finished — menu, prices, hours, the lot — and vendors.json is plain text, so a door I have not got to yet is a coordinate and a name away from working.


<b>🛒 HOW YOU BUY IT</b>

Walk up and press <b>E</b>. A counter or a shelf opens a list with tabs, a picture and a price on every row, and a line about each one. Some shops have a door as well — hold E instead of tapping it and you go inside.

<ul>
<li>🌭 <b>A hot dog cart just hands you a hot dog</b>, and he eats it on the spot.</li>
<li>🚗 <b>Drive-throughs work from the driver's seat.</b> A meal at the wheel is eaten slowly between junctions instead of in four seconds at the window, and the horn is held off while you are ordering.</li>
<li>🎒 <b>What you buy goes in your pocket</b> by default — five things, on <b>F12</b> — so a shop is somewhere you go back to rather than a place you stand and eat. Turn up with full pockets and he eats it there and then rather than refusing the sale.</li>
<li>🧊 <b>Every safehouse kitchen has a working fridge</b> that holds forty things. Your pocket on one side, the fridge on the other, one key to move things across and another to eat from either side. It is found by looking for a fridge rather than by a coordinate, so interior mods work too.</li>
</ul>


<b>⚠️ IT COSTS SOMETHING TO IGNORE</b>

Sprinting empties all three faster than walking, and the gauges visibly speed up while you do it, so the reason is on screen.

<ul>
<li>🍗 <b>Let hunger reach nothing</b> and it starts costing health, slowly, with a warning first. It stops well above the floor — it will never kill you.</li>
<li>💧 <b>Let thirst run low</b> and it takes your <i>wind</i> before anything else: the sprint meter empties more than twice as fast, so you lose your legs halfway across a forecourt. Then he starts breathing hard — in his own voice, out of the game's own sounds — and the rate of it tells you how bad it is without looking at the bar. Thirst empties in a game day against hunger's two and a bit, so it is usually the one that gets you first, and a can fixes it.</li>
<li>🌙 <b>Let sleep reach nothing</b> and the picture starts to swim. Stay up through that and you collapse where you stand and wake up four hours later.</li>
</ul>

Sleep in any bed, and how well you sleep depends on whose bed it is. A car is worse than a safehouse and better than nothing. Sleeping also sobers you up, because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.

<b>A beer is a drink and a whisky is not.</b> Anything wet fills the thirst bar, food fills a little of it, and alcohol subtracts on a curve — so a pint is genuinely hydrating and a spirit is very nearly a net loss. Nothing on the shelf had to be told which it was; it falls out of one rule.


<b>🔧 YOURS TO CHANGE</b>

213 settings across seven tabs in an in-game menu on <b>F11</b>, each with a line saying what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes written back to the ini without disturbing your comments or your ordering.

Every shop, every item and every picture is a plain file. Add a shop, change a price, write a menu, drop a PNG in the icons folder and that item has a picture. Anything the game cannot find is skipped with a line in the log rather than breaking the mod.

<b>🖥️ Ultrawide, or a HUD mod that moves your radar?</b> Map left/right and Map up/down under Vitals drag the frame, the bars and the fuel gauge to wherever your map actually is.


<b>📱 IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH</b>

The shops talk on its social feed — 71 accounts and 475 lines, written as people who sell lunch rather than as gang members.

And its drugs do something to the meters. A stimulant pins the energy bar at the top and then costs you a comedown; two bars of a sedative and the screen starts going out for a second at a time, with no warning and with your controls still yours, because where you have got to by the time it comes back is the point. Acid gets twelve minutes of the game's own peyote look, swelling on two periods that do not divide into each other so it never settles into a filter and never falls into a rhythm.

All of it is entirely optional, entirely one-way, and completely silent if you do not have it.


<b>💾 INSTALL</b>

Drag into your GTA V folder, say yes to merging, load a save. Needs ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet 3.6 or newer. Works on Legacy and Enhanced from the same files. No game file is modified and nothing is written to your save — delete three things and it never happened.

<b>❗ Unblock the ZIP before you extract it</b>, or ScriptHookVDotNet will refuse to load the DLL and say nothing about it. If you already run script mods, take the plain zip — the -full one bundles its own ScriptHookVDotNet and two of those fight.


<b>⌨️ KEYS</b>

<ul>
<li><b>E</b> — interact: the counter, the bed and the car alike. Hold it at a door to go in.</li>
<li><b>F11</b> — settings</li>
<li><b>F12</b> — your pocket</li>
<li><b>Scroll Lock</b> — fly the map and find every vending machine. You only ever want this once.</li>
</ul>

All four can be changed in the ini.

🎮 <b>On a controller:</b> the context button does everything E does. The panels open on a chord, because a pad has no button spare — <b>LB + D-pad Up</b> for settings, <b>RB + A</b> for your pocket. Inside a panel the d-pad moves, A chooses and B closes, and the on-screen hints switch to those glyphs the moment you pick a pad up.
