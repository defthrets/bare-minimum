0.1.0  --  first release
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HUNGER AND SLEEP, as two small marks beside the minimap rather than two bars.
  Each goes through five drawings as it empties -- an apple eaten down to a
  core, an eye closing -- so the state reads from the shape at a glance. A bar
  style is in the settings for anybody who wants one.

  Both run on the GAME clock, so a day of them is about 48 minutes of play.

  108 PLACES TO BUY FOOD, and they are the real ones: every 24/7 and LTD, the
  taco windows, the hot dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Bean Machine, Cool
  Beans, Rob's Liquor, Taco Bomb, Pizza This, Cluckin' Bell's walk-up, the
  liquor stores, the corner shops, the delis, and the restaurants nobody ever
  gets to walk into. Del Perro and Vespucci are covered end to end.

  157 THINGS TO EAT across three categories, each with its own price, its own
  effect and its own line about itself.

  SHOPS WITH A MENU AND STALLS WITH ONE KEY. A counter or a shelf opens a list
  with tabs, pictures and prices. A hot dog cart just hands you a hot dog. The
  drive-throughs work from the driver's seat, and a meal at the wheel is eaten
  slowly between junctions rather than in four seconds at the window.

  A POCKET, on F11. What you buy goes in it by default -- three things -- so a
  shop is somewhere you go back to rather than a place you stand and eat.

  A FRIDGE IN EVERY SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN. Forty slots, your pocket on the left and
  the fridge on the right, one key to move things across and another to eat
  from either side. Found by looking for a fridge rather than by a coordinate,
  so every kitchen the base game put one in works.

  SLEEPING IN ANY BED, with the quality depending on whose bed it is. A car is
  worse than a safehouse and better than nothing. Sleeping sobers you up,
  because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.

  RUNNING COSTS YOU. Sprinting empties both meters faster, and the marks
  visibly speed up while you do it, so the reason is on screen rather than in a
  changelog.

  EMPTY MEANS SOMETHING. Hunger at nothing starts taking health, slowly, with a
  warning first, and stops well above the floor -- it will never kill you.
  Sleep at nothing makes the picture swim, and staying up through that puts you
  on the floor where you stand for four hours.

  DRINK, AND GET DRUNK. Beer, wine and spirits carry their own effect, the walk
  goes with it, and sleeping it off works for free.

  A SETTINGS MENU ON F7. Ninety-seven settings across nine tabs, each with a
  line saying what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes
  written back to the ini without touching your comments or your ordering.

  EVERYTHING IS DATA. The shops, the food, the brands and the social posts are
  four json files in scripts\BareMinimum\. Add a shop, change a price, write a
  menu. Anything the game cannot find is skipped with a line in the log.

  IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH, the shops talk on its feed: 71 accounts and 475
  lines, written as people who sell lunch rather than as gang members. Entirely
  optional, entirely one-way, and completely silent without it.

  Works on GTA V Legacy and Enhanced from the same files. No game file is
  modified and your save is never touched.
