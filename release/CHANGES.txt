0.1.1  --  the bars ship, and the three of them are told apart properly
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE BARS ARE THE HUD NOW. They were always in the box and you had to go and
  find them, and the first thing anybody reported was thinking the icons WERE
  the bug. The icons have not been touched and are still one press away --
  F7 -> HUD -> HUD style -- they are simply no longer the thing you get without
  asking.

  IT SHIPS SET UP THE WAY IT IS PLAYED. The gauges' length, width, mark size,
  wave and drift, the pantry at five slots, the hunger and sleep clocks, and
  shop markers on the pause map are all real numbers off a real install rather
  than the first ones that looked about right.

  MICHAEL, FRANKLIN AND TREVOR WERE LABELLED WRONG, and had been since the
  first build. The three model hashes were each a person out of step, and
  Michael's was not in the list at all -- so Franklin's hunger was filed under
  "michael", Trevor's under "franklin", and Michael's under a raw hash. Nothing
  was ever shared or lost between them, because nothing reads the name; every
  label was simply wrong, in the log and in three save files.

  YOUR SAVE IS CORRECTED ONCE, ON LOAD. needs.json, pantry.json and fridge.json
  are renamed in place the first time 0.1.1 runs and stamped version 2, so it
  cannot happen twice. Nobody inherits anybody else's stomach on the way past.


0.1.0  --  first release, and a work in progress
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  WHAT IS NOT FINISHED, first, because it is the honest way round: the shop map.
  108 doors are in and they are complete ones, but the convenience stores and
  the fuel stations are thin, and more go in with every update. Everything below
  this line works today.

  HUNGER AND SLEEP, as two thin gauges low on the screen. They are animated and
  they move differently on purpose: hunger churns and gets more agitated the
  emptier it is, sleep breathes and the breath slows as you tire.

  A SECOND HUD IN THE BOX. Switch HUD style to ICONS and the bars come off,
  leaving an apple and a moon that go through five drawings each as they empty
  -- the apple eaten to a core, the eye closing. It reads as a shape rather
  than a length and tells you less on purpose. One press in the F7 menu.

  THEY SIT LOW AND OUT PAST THE RIGHT-HAND EDGE OF THE MAP, and they stay put.
  Both HUDs share the spot, so switching style does not move anything.
  There is an automatic placement that hugs the minimap and it is switched off,
  because it has to ask the game where the map ends and the game answers for a
  STOCK radar -- run anything that has resized yours and the marks land on top
  of it. F7 moves them anywhere you like, live, as you press.

  Both run on the GAME clock, so a day of them is about 48 minutes of play.

  108 PLACES TO BUY FOOD, and they are the real ones: the taco windows, the hot
  dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Bean Machine, Cool Beans, Rob's Liquor,
  Taco Bomb, Pizza This, thirteen liquor stores, the corner shops, the delis,
  and the restaurants nobody ever gets to walk into. Del Perro and Vespucci are
  covered end to end.

  157 THINGS TO EAT across three categories, each with its own price, its own
  effect and its own line about itself.

  SHOPS WITH A MENU AND STALLS WITH ONE KEY. A counter or a shelf opens a list
  with tabs, pictures and prices. A hot dog cart just hands you a hot dog. The
  drive-throughs work from the driver's seat, and a meal at the wheel is eaten
  slowly between junctions rather than in four seconds at the window.

  A POCKET, on F11. What you buy goes in it by default -- three things -- so a
  shop is somewhere you go back to rather than a place you stand and eat.

  A FRIDGE IN EVERY SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN. Forty slots, your pocket on the left and
  the fridge on the right, one key to move things across and another to eat
  from either side. Found by looking for a fridge rather than by a coordinate,
  so every kitchen the base game put one in works.

  SLEEPING IN ANY BED, with the quality depending on whose bed it is. A car is
  worse than a safehouse and better than nothing. Sleeping sobers you up,
  because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.

  RUNNING COSTS YOU. Sprinting empties both meters faster, and the marks
  visibly speed up while you do it, so the reason is on screen rather than in a
  changelog.

  EMPTY MEANS SOMETHING. Hunger at nothing starts taking health, slowly, with a
  warning first, and stops well above the floor -- it will never kill you.
  Sleep at nothing makes the picture swim, and staying up through that puts you
  on the floor where you stand for four hours.

  DRINK, AND GET DRUNK. Beer, wine and spirits carry their own effect, the walk
  goes with it, and sleeping it off works for free.

  A SETTINGS MENU ON F7. Ninety-seven settings across nine tabs, each with a
  line saying what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes
  written back to the ini without touching your comments or your ordering.

  EVERYTHING IS DATA. The shops, the food, the brands and the social posts are
  four json files in scripts\BareMinimum\. Add a shop, change a price, write a
  menu. Anything the game cannot find is skipped with a line in the log.

  IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH, the shops talk on its feed: 71 accounts and 475
  lines, written as people who sell lunch rather than as gang members. Entirely
  optional, entirely one-way, and completely silent without it.

  Works on GTA V Legacy and Enhanced from the same files. No game file is
  modified and your save is never touched.
