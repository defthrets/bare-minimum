0.9.0  --  THE BODIES MOVE IN, A REAL BAG, AND A BENCH TO SLEEP ON
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE DEAD ARE THIS MOD'S NOW. Searching a body used to live in Posted Up and
  carrying one in Five0 Patrol, so the food on a corpse was only there if you
  ran the gang mod, and two mods reached for the same body. Both are here.
  Stand over a body and hold E to search it -- cash, food and drink, and drugs
  as well if Posted Up 0.9.9 or newer is installed -- then hold E again to pick
  it up and carry it. Guns are the game's own again: they fall to the ground
  when somebody dies, as they always did, rather than sitting on the card.
  Settings on the F11 menu under BODIES. Asked for by NordCyborg.

  IF YOU RUN FIVE0 PATROL: its current download (0.2.1) still has a body carry
  of its own, and its next one will not. Until then, turn one of them off --
  [Bodies] Carry = false in this mod's ini is the one this end controls.

  A REAL BAG ON HIS BACK. The bag Posted Up gives you was a strap across the
  chest -- one drawable, the same for every bag, and missing on half the tops
  in the game. It is a real bag model now: fifteen of the game's own, picked on
  the F11 menu under BAG, the tactical pack by default. Every one has its own
  place on his back, and the position tool on the same page moves it a
  centimetre at a time on the numpad and saves it.

  AND THE BAG YOU PUT DOWN IS THE BAG YOU CHOSE, not a duffel. Each one has
  its own place on the ground too, set with the same tool while it lies there.
  Needs Posted Up 0.9.9 or newer, which is the mod the bag belongs to.

  SLEEP ON ANYTHING YOU COULD SLEEP ON. Fifteen of the nineteen bed names this
  mod looked for did not exist in the game -- written from memory and never
  checked -- so a bed was only ever found by four models: Michael's, Jimmy's,
  Trevor's and Lester's. Franklin's two beds never worked. Every name is read
  out of the game's own object list now, in three kinds:

      beds       56   a full night, 8 hours, and a little health back
      couches   112   sofas, couches, loungers: 6 hours, four fifths of it
      rough      72   park and bus-stop benches, tents, homeless shelters,
                      mattresses and sleeping bags on the ground: 5 hours

  A couch or a bench offers once you stop at it, not as you walk past. A bed
  that a housing or interior mod stands up and this still does not know goes
  in [Sleeping] ExtraModels. Reported by Vitalezzzz (M8T's Home Ownership
  houses) and asked for by Jcats. How good each kind is, and whether couches
  and rough sleeping are on at all, is on the F11 menu under SLEEP.

  FRANKLIN'S FRIDGE ON FORUM DRIVE. The game's own "drink beer" is bolted to
  that fridge and always got the button first. A fridge of this mod's own now
  stands in the same spot, and it only offers when you are right at it. Found
  by D_Cypher003, reported by NordCyborg.

  THE FOOD STAYS WHERE IT WAS FITTED. Everything sat right in his hand while it
  was being eaten and wrong the moment his arm came down -- between bites, and
  in the pocket while you chose it, where it was in the wrong hand as well. The
  animation moves the bone the food hangs from; the mod measures by how much
  and holds the food off his wrist in the same place relative to his palm. The
  fitting bench can still set an arm-down position for any one item by hand.
  Asked for by GTA5gamers.

  THREE BITES, AND THE EMPTY DROPS FROM HIS SIDE. A meal or a drink is three
  passes of the animation with a pause between each, then his arm comes down
  and the can or wrapper leaves his hand there, not at his mouth. Drinks were
  being cut short by their own timer and never got the third sip; they do now.

  SMOKING. The animation stops when the cigarette does. The lit end smoulders
  the whole time it is in his hand, and he rests between drags instead of
  taking them back to back -- [Eating] SmokeBreakSeconds, 3 by default.

  YOUR INI IS NOT REPLACED. Everything new takes its default until you copy
  its lines over from the ini in this download.


0.8.4  --  THE MARKERS COME BACK, AND THE BARBER STOPS SELLING SANDWICHES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE VENDING MACHINE AND FRUIT STALL MARKERS COME BACK WHEN THE SWITCH DOES.
  Turning "Shop map markers" off and on again took every remembered machine off
  the map -- a hundred-odd of them -- until the next script reload. Clear's own

  names on that list that have never been seen in a log are marked as such
  rather than trusted.

  AND THE LOG SAYS MORE, so the next one of these is found from evidence.
  Loading the remembered machines now names the file, the rows, what was
  dropped and how many markers were actually made. A marker the game refuses to
  create is said once instead of swallowed. Saving a setting names the setting.
  A counter in a shop the mod has not seen before writes that shop's scripts,
  once per shop.

  One thing this download does NOT settle: since a reload this morning the

0.8.3  --  BUYING FALLS THROUGH TO THE BAG, LIKE LOOTING ALWAYS DID
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  "NO ROOM IN YOUR POCKETS, SO YOU ATE IT" -- WITH A BAG OF EMPTY SLOTS ON HIS
  BACK. Reported as the mod saying he had no room when he plainly did, and that
  is exactly what it was doing.

  There were two rules and they disagreed. Looting a body -- what the mod next
  door hands over -- has always tried the pocket and then the bag. The shop
  counter and the street vendors only ever tried the pocket: they asked whether
  it was full, got yes, and handed him the burger to eat standing there. Once
  drugs started taking places in 0.7.1, one packet and three bags of product
  fill a pocket, and every purchase after that was a meal at the counter.

  ONE RULE NOW: the pocket, then the bag on his back. The counter, the vendors
  and the loot all ask the same thing. The shop checks there is room ANYWHERE
  before it takes your money, so a full pocket with no bag is still a meal on
  the spot rather than a refund. The message says which it went in -- "in your
  pocket" or "in your bag" -- and counts the same number the F12 header counts.

  Kit is the exception on purpose. A bong is carried in the POCKET or it does
  not count as carried, so it never goes in the bag.

  AND TWO MORE FROM READING THE DAY'S CHANGES BACK. Turning off "Drugs in
  pocket" hid the drug tiles but went on charging for them, so invisible drugs
  were eating slots nobody could see; the count now obeys the same switch the
  screen does. And one call to the game clock was sitting outside the guard it
  belonged in.


0.8.2  --  THE HUD SAYS WHY IT IS NOT THERE, AND A CRASH THAT WAS WAITING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE HUD NOW WRITES DOWN WHY IT IS HIDDEN. It always knew -- it works the
  reason out every frame -- and it only ever said so when the row FLAPPED four
  times in ten seconds. A HUD that goes once and stays gone tripped no counter
  and printed nothing at all, which is the state anybody actually reports. So a
  session that loses the bars now has two lines in the log with timestamps on
  them:

      The HUD is not being drawn because <reason>.
      The HUD is back: <reason> is no longer true.

  Diagnostic only. It reads the answer and writes it down; it decides nothing,
  changes no condition and cannot itself take the row away.

  A NULL KEY THAT WOULD HAVE THROWN. The packet counter added in 0.8.0 keyed its
  table on which character you are playing WITHOUT the guard its two neighbours
  in that file both have -- so a session where the pocket screen drew before
  anything else had touched the store would have handed a null to a Dictionary
  and taken the tick down with it. Found by reading the file against the methods
  either side of it. All three guard it now.

  THE 0.8.0 CHANGE TO WHEN THE ROW HIDES IS OUT. It made a hidden game HUD only
  count while the map had also stopped drawing, which is sound reasoning and was
  shipped on reasoning alone rather than on a test. It is back to what 0.7.1 did
  until the log above proves what the right rule is.

  So the bars vanishing in particular places -- reported by NordCyborg and
  Blackheart117 -- is NOT fixed in this build. It is the next thing, and the two
  lines above are how it gets found properly instead of guessed at.

0.8.0  --  FIVE FROM THE COMMENTS, AND THE SHOPS GET THEIR SHELVES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE ULTRAWIDE MAP OFFSET SURVIVES A RESTART. It was never being lost -- the
  ini kept the number the whole time. It was never being APPLIED: the call that
  moves the frame ran seventy-four lines before the setting was read, so it
  moved by nought, and nudging the row in the menu was the only thing that ever
  told it. Which is why it worked until you reloaded, and why making the file
  read-only could not help. Reported by BoBo-Acilia and Blackheart117.

  THE BARS STOP VANISHING IN THE STREET. Both reporters said the same thing --
  the minimap stayed up while the row went -- and that was the whole clue.
  Anything can ask the game to hide its own HUD and the scripts around a mission
  trigger do exactly that, but this row lives beside the radar rather than
  inside the game's HUD, so it was obeying a flag that had nothing to do with
  it. A hidden HUD over a LIVE map is somebody switching off the game's own
  furniture; a cutscene, a fade and the pause menu stop the map drawing too, so
  every deliberate blackout still hides it. Reported by NordCyborg and
  Blackheart117.

  FRANKLIN'S FRIDGE WORKS. The game keeps its own beer interaction against that
  one fridge at Forum Drive and it reads the same button the prompt advertises,
  so whoever got there first won and usually it was the game -- which is exactly
  why Michael's always worked and Franklin's never did. The prompt holds the
  button while it is on screen, the way the shop counter has since it shipped.
  Diagnosed in the comments by D_Cypher003, who spotted it is only the kitchen
  with the beer in it.

  MARKERS ON PAUSE MAP NOW MEANS ONE THING. It only ever applied to the shops,
  so turning it off cleared those off the pause map and left every vending
  machine and fruit stall on it. Reported by D_Cypher003.

  A PACKET OF CIGARETTES IS TWENTY CIGARETTES. The tile said Redwood, the icon
  was a packet, and twelve dollars bought one smoke. It cannot be done by
  selling twenty, because the pocket counts units against slots and a
  twenty-pack would be the whole pocket -- so an item says how many uses are in
  one, and a packet is ONE thing in the pocket with a count on it. The tile
  counts smokes rather than packets. Reported by User20192910291.

  THE COLD SECTION AND THE AISLES SELL WHAT IS IN THEM. A 24/7 was a room with a
  counter in it and four walls of stock you could not touch. Stand at the beer
  wall and buy a beer, at the dairy wall and buy the milk, at the shelving and
  buy the tins. The counter still sells everything and still wins whenever it is
  in reach -- the aisles are looked for last and only at arm's length, so the
  only way to get the fridge is to be stood at the fridge.

  Which retail chiller is beer and which is milk cannot be read off a model
  name; the game dresses the same fridge either way depending on where it
  stands. So the log names the model the first time you open one, and
  [Counters] AisleDrinks and AisleFood move it between groups without a rebuild.


0.7.1  --  A DRUG IN YOUR POCKET TAKES A PLACE IN YOUR POCKET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  If you run Hoodrich, the drugs were drawn in the pocket grid and counted for
  nothing -- five tiles sat over a header reading 2 of 5, and you could fill all
  five food slots on top of them. A pocket with eight things in it is not a
  pocket.

  One place per KIND, which is what the grid draws: one tile for the weed
  however many grams, the same as one tile for the crisps however many packets.
  The grams stay Hoodrich's rule and are still reported beside the count.

  It does not shrink your slot count -- that is your setting, and a pocket that
  quietly reports a smaller cap than the one you typed is one nobody can reason
  about. And it does not affect putting something back: returning an item the
  mod took out of your hand has always ignored the cap and still does, or a
  pocket that filled with drugs mid-meal would eat the sandwich.


0.7.0  --  ANIMATION, BREAK, ANIMATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  EATING AND DRINKING GET A BEAT IN THE MIDDLE. The same clip was rolled back to
  back for the length of the meal, which reads as a man winding a handle -- the
  same motion three or four times with no pause between them. Two passes now
  with a rest in the middle, holding the thing, which is the shape of taking a
  bite and then taking another. [Eating] Bites and BreakSeconds.

  AND THE MEAL ENDS WITH THE LAST PASS, so what you watch and how long it takes
  are one thing instead of two numbers that have to be kept in step. Nothing
  loops any more: every clip runs its own length and ends, which also kills the
  old drink jank for good -- loop_bottle is already a loop, and looping a loop
  restarts it against a blend every pass.

  Smoking is untouched. It is three different clips in a deliberate order rather
  than one clip played twice, and a rest inside that would be a man stopping
  halfway through raising a cigarette.

  DROPPED LITTER FALLS. A prop made by the game arrives with its rigid body
  asleep -- the physics engine parks bodies that are not moving, and one that
  has never moved has never been woken -- so it hung in the air where it
  appeared, and the little push it was given went to a body nothing was
  simulating. Which is why it read as frozen rather than slow.

  A BAG OF CRISPS IS A BAG OF CRISPS. Its model list led with the Burger Shot
  fries carton, which is still right for French Fries and was never right here.


0.6.0  --  A FITTING BENCH, AND THE HAND IS A SWITCH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  WHERE A THING SITS IN HIS HAND IS SOMETHING YOU SET YOURSELF NOW, in the game,
  and it sticks. A screen that holds the model in his hand in the pose he eats
  it in, and lets you walk it into place: three axes of position, three of
  rotation, a finer step on CTRL. Written to the ini the moment you lock it in,
  per MODEL rather than per item, so everything holding a burger moves when you
  move the burger.

  IT PICKS THE MODEL TOO, out of the game's own object list -- 1,578 holdable
  models -- rather than the sixty the shops already use. The Pizza Slice was
  holding a closed pizza box because the six pizza models this game has were
  never on the list to choose from.

  AND IT CAN FILL IN THE REST. Every unfitted model takes the numbers off the
  fitted one whose name is most like it: a cup from a cup, a tin from a tin. A
  guess, and a far better start than nought.

  WHICH HAND HE EATS WITH IS A SWITCH. It moves the ANIMATION and the prop
  together, because they cannot be split -- otherwise he raises an empty fist
  while the food rides on the other hip. Nothing in the game's own name lists
  records which arm a clip raises, so this is the only honest way to settle it:
  press it, look at him, and it is saved.

  THE PANEL STANDS OFF TO THE SIDE, so it is not on top of the man you are
  looking at.

  BONGS AT THE SMOKE SHOPS, and weed smoked with one plays Franklin's own bong
  animation. He breathes out real cigarette smoke rather than the fire
  extinguisher plume that shipped by mistake, and any animation stops if he
  draws a weapon, shoots or throws a punch.

  VENDING MACHINES PUT THE DRINK IN YOUR POCKET instead of him drinking it where
  he stands. One seller per cart, where two were spawning on the same stand.

  FRANKLIN'S FRIDGE AT AUNT DENISE'S IS FOUND, and so are beds and counters that
  were being filtered out by a check that asks whether a model can be SPAWNED --
  the wrong question entirely for a fixture that is already part of the map.

  TWO GRIDS WHEN A BAG IS WORN, so things move between the bag and your five
  pocket slots, and F12 closes it the way it opens it.

  ALL 201 ITEM PICTURES AUDITED, the wrong ones redrawn, and nothing in the
  shops draws a blank white shape any more. The icon set costs a quarter of the
  video memory it did and looks better for it.


0.5.0  --  HE FINISHES WHAT HE BUYS, AND LEAVES IT ON THE PAVEMENT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HE DROPS THE EMPTY. A can that vanishes out of a man's hand the instant he
  stops drinking was the one moment this mod stopped modelling anything. Now he
  drops it and it stays where it falls -- a can for a drink, a bottle for a beer,
  and whatever he ate it off where he ate it off something: a bowl of chilli
  leaves a bowl, a slice of pie leaves the plate, a sundae leaves the glass. A
  slice with nothing under it in its own picture leaves the wrapper instead,
  which is judged per item because only the picture knows.

  Capped at twelve and the oldest goes when the newest lands -- props are a
  limited resource in this game and nothing else was going to take them away.
  A reload does not sweep them up; they are handed back to the game, which
  clears them on its own terms like any other rubbish in the world.

  HE ACTUALLY SMOKES IT. The smoking dictionary holds three clips and the mod
  played the first on a loop -- which is the one where he stands there holding
  it. All three are played now, one after another, for twenty seconds, and he
  can walk about while he does. The clip itself says when it has finished, so
  none of the three had to be timed by hand.

  A VENDING MACHINE IS NOT A SHOP. He drinks it standing at the thing, the way
  the game always has, instead of putting it in his pocket. And the machine's own
  reach-and-press gets to finish first: it was being replaced on the very next
  line, so the press never played and the can appeared in his hand out of air.

  BLACK OUT AT THE WHEEL AND THE POLICE ALWAYS COME. The kerbside wake-up is a
  gamble on purpose -- you chose to park in the street and sleep. Passing out is
  not that: you did not choose the spot, the car is stopped dead in a lane you
  were driving down, and that gets found. They ask what happened rather than
  telling you to move along.

  TWO SWITCHES FOR THE BARS' MOVEMENT, one for each half of it: what lives inside
  the fill, and the fill's own surface. There were sliders for both and a slider
  at 0 is not off -- one stopped the specks moving and still drew them, the other
  flattened the wobble and left the jolt. Turning the insides off also costs the
  frame fewer rectangles, which is a real lever for anyone fighting the ceiling.

  AND THE HUD'S DECORATION COMES BACK. One frame over the shared rectangle line
  -- a menu opening -- priced the decoration at three times what it costs and the
  release test could then never be met, so the cells, crumbs, bubbles and streaks
  went for the rest of the session. Measured on an ordinary frame now, and a trim
  needs three frames in a row rather than one.


0.4.1  --  THE GAME'S HEALTH STRIP STAYS HIDDEN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE GAME'S OWN HEALTH AND ARMOUR STRIP COULD COME BACK under the map and stay.
  The layout that hides it was asked for once and then again only on a handful
  of moments -- a pause, a fade, a switch, a cutscene -- and the game undid it on
  a moment that was not one of those, with no re-ask to follow. The log for the
  session shows it: the ask, the rebuild, then nothing for three minutes while
  the strip was back on screen.

  SIX MORE MOMENTS ARE WATCHED, and every re-ask is written to the log WITH THE
  MOMENT THAT CAUSED IT, so if it ever comes back again the log names the trigger
  instead of leaving it to guesswork. In and out of a vehicle, the HUD hidden and
  given back, the wanted level, an interior boundary, a phone call ending.

  AND A QUIET HEARTBEAT: every five seconds while no waypoint is set. Asking
  every frame was what made the GPS route strobe, but with no route on the map
  there is nothing to disturb, so the ask is free. It puts a five-second ceiling
  on how long the strip can stay back whenever you are not navigating, and it
  stands down the moment a waypoint goes up, so the strobe cannot return.

  Nothing else changed. If you have 0.4.0 and the strip has not come back, this
  is not worth the download -- but it will, and then it is.

0.3.1  --  HINDI IN LETTERS THE GAME CAN DRAW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HINDI IS ROMANISED NOW, and it is the one on the menu because it is the one
  that draws. 0.3.0 shipped it in Devanagari, every word of it correct, and it
  came out as a row of empty boxes -- GTA V has never had a Hindi release, so
  there is no font in the game with those letters in it. That is the most
  useless kind of correct.

  Latin goes through the same font as English and works on every install, and
  romanised Hindi is not a compromise invented here: it is how a great deal of
  Hindi is actually typed. All 120 phrases, checked to hold nothing the game's
  font does not have.

  The Devanagari is kept whole as hi-Deva, and works the day somebody puts a
  font in the game that has it. Its row cannot be written in its own letters --
  that is the whole problem -- so it is labelled in Latin and says why it did
  not draw if you pick it.

  Nothing else changed. If 0.3.0 is running and you do not read Hindi, this is
  not worth the download.


0.3.0  --  TEN LANGUAGES, AND THE HUD STOPS BLINKING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TEN LANGUAGES, off the first row of the settings menu: English in British and
  American, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, French, German, Russian, Polish,
  Chinese and Hindi. Every prompt, every screen, every tab and every row label.

  ANYTHING A TRANSLATION HAS NOT COVERED SHOWS IN ENGLISH, and that is the
  design rather than a shortfall. A language file is a plain map from what the
  mod says to what it should say instead -- there are no identifiers in it --
  so a miss hands back the English and there is no such thing as a missing
  string. A half-finished file is a working mod in two languages.

  Which also means the food translates the same way the buttons do. A hot dog's
  name and the joke under it go through the same four functions as everything
  else, so filling them in is editing a text file, not building anything.

  lang\en-GB.json is the template. Copy it, fill in the right-hand side, save it
  under your own code. No rebuild and no tools.

  WHETHER THE LETTERS DRAW IS A SEPARATE QUESTION FROM WHETHER THE WORDS EXIST,
  and the log says which at load. Latin and Cyrillic are in the game's fonts
  always, so six of the ten are safe anywhere. Chinese rides in on the font GTA
  loads for its OWN language, so on an English game expect boxes. Devanagari is
  not in the game at all -- there has never been a Hindi GTA V -- so Hindi will
  almost certainly be boxes until a font with it is installed. The text is real
  either way.

  THE AIR METER IS BACK. The game's own breath bar is drawn in the same part of
  the minimap as its health and armour strip, so the layout that hides those was
  hiding it too, and it took going swimming to find out. It cannot be let back on
  its own -- one layout covers all three -- so it is drawn instead, in exactly
  the space the original used, and only while you are under the water. The full
  mark is measured rather than assumed, so a diving ability or a rebreather is
  accounted for without being told about. It beats when it is nearly out, and
  faster the less is left.

  THE HUD STOPPED BLINKING. The whole row was going off and on, everywhere, all
  the time. Four files each asked the game whether its HUD was hidden, each
  asked fresh every time it drew, and each took everything down the instant the
  answer came back no -- and that native is a THIS FRAME one, which the game
  uses for cutscenes and phone calls and which any other script can call too.
  One script doing it on one frame in ten took the bars down on one frame in
  ten. There is one answer now, asked once a frame, and the twitchy tests have
  to hold for six frames before anything goes.

  THE MINIMAP STOPPED FLICKERING THE GPS. The call that hides the game's health
  strip is not a switch being flipped, it is a re-layout of the minimap, and it
  was being made sixty times a second. The map's own picture survives that; the
  GPS route and the turn arrow do not, because they are built by that movie only
  while a route is set. Asked once now, and again only when something has had a
  chance to undo it.

  AND THE HOT DOGS WERE BEING DRUNK. Four of them carried an animation whose
  name says eat, which sits in the eating family, and which is a bottle going to
  the mouth. Five bar drinks had the opposite problem and were being chewed.

0.2.0  --  A THIRD NEED, THE VITALS MOVE IN, AND THE DRUGS BITE BACK

  THIRST. A third meter, standing in the row with the other two and behaving
  exactly as they do -- 100 is fine, 0 is trouble -- and what makes it its own
  thing is the speed and the price. Twenty-four game hours from full to dry,
  which is well under half of hunger's, so it empties about as often as you
  sleep; and one can fixes it, where a day's food is several meals. That ratio
  is the whole point: a meter that emptied on hunger's clock would be a second
  hunger bar you fix at the same counter, and it would tell you nothing.

  WHAT FILLS IT IS WORKED OUT, NOT TYPED. Every item in the shop gets a figure
  from one rule -- a drink is most of the bar, food carries across a fifth of
  its own hunger value, and ALCOHOL SUBTRACTS, on a curve, so a beer is a drink
  and a whisky is very much not. A hundred hand-written numbers would have been
  a hundred chances to get one wrong and no chance of noticing.

  AND BEING DRY IS FELT BEFORE IT IS SEEN. It does not slow you down -- three
  needs each multiplying your speed is a man walking at a third of it with
  nothing on screen saying why. It takes your WIND: the sprint meter empties up
  to two and a half times faster, on a bar already in the row, so the cause and
  the effect are two columns apart. Then he starts breathing hard, in his own
  voice, out of the game's own non-verbal set -- and the rate of it is the
  reading, faster the worse it gets and faster again while he runs.

  THE DRUGS COST SOMETHING NOW. A stimulant pinned the energy bar at the top for
  minutes and then simply let go. Now there is a comedown: a few minutes moving
  like a man who has run himself into the ground, with a grunt on the way in.
  Two bars of a sedative and the screen starts GOING OUT for a second at a time,
  with no warning and no loss of control -- where you have got to by the time it
  comes back is the mechanic. And LSD is the one drug here that is not
  arithmetic: twelve minutes of the game's own peyote timecycle, swelling on two
  periods that do not divide into each other so it never settles into a filter
  and never falls into a rhythm.

  HEALTH IS RED THE WHOLE WAY, vivid at full and dark at nothing, so every bar
  in the row now reads by one rule: brightness says how bad, hue says which
  meter. The low-health beat goes BLACK rather than white -- on a red bar going
  pale reads as the level rising -- which leaves white to mean one thing, and it
  means it the instant you are hit, armour included.

  EVERY VENDING MACHINE YOU WALK PAST STAYS ON THE MAP. They were keyed on the
  prop's handle, which is the game's memory of a streamed object and not the
  machine, so they vanished the moment you drove off and came back as new ones.
  They are remembered by position now, and kept between sessions. There is a
  map survey on the F7 page that will fly the whole lot for you.

  ---


  THE MENU GOES OVER THE BRIDGE. Another mod could be handed food and could take
  it away, but had no way to ask what food EXISTS or what any of it costs -- so
  it could not offer you a shelf. Two more calls on the API: what a counter
  would sell, and the price of one. Nothing about this changes anything in this
  mod; it is what Posted Up's delivery orders from.

  THE ROW STOPPED TAKING OTHER MODS' PANELS OFF THE SCREEN. Rectangles come
  out of one list the game keeps for the whole machine, and once it is full
  everything handed over after that is dropped -- so the script that pays for
  a busy frame is whichever draws LAST, whatever it drew. Five bars, each
  filling its gradient with a band every four pixels, were most of that list
  on their own: another mod's phone came up in a car with no background at
  all, because its screen is a rectangle and there was no room left for it.
  A band every fourteen pixels now, which on a bar this narrow looks the
  same, and nothing under half a pixel is drawn at all. The row also counts
  what it spends and writes the worst frame to the log, so the next time
  something goes missing there is a number to look at.

  VITALS IS PART OF BARE MINIMUM NOW. The health, armour and special ability
  bars that were a mod of their own stand in the row with the sleep and food
  bars: five columns, one frame -- the same surround, the same channel, the same
  plate and mark under every one, drawn through the same code, so the row is one
  instrument. The order is armour, health, sleep, food, energy -- or whatever
  [HUD] RowOrder says. The game's own
  strip under the minimap goes, and comes back the moment the script unloads or
  the vitals are switched off. Vitals.dll and Vitals.ini should be removed if
  you have them; the two would fight over the same bars.

  THE THIRD BAR IS ENERGY. It drains while you sprint, and empty it locks the
  sprint to a tired jog, the way the game's own stamina does, until it has come
  back a third of the way. It refills on its own in seconds, faster stood still or in a car than at a jog. The
  special ability shows through it: the bar lights and beats while the ability
  runs. Energy = false in [Vitals] puts the special ability's charge back on it.

  THE COLOURS. The stock bars keep their own -- health is green and runs down
  through amber to red with the level, armour is blue, energy is yellow -- and
  the two that were here changed to keep clear of them: food is orange now, not
  green, and sleep is purple, not blue.

  A FRAME ROUND THE MINIMAP, in the bars' own black, with a plate under the map
  where the game's strip was -- and the plate always says where you are, the
  street on the left and the suburb on the right. The game's own area and
  street names are hidden while it is on. [Minimap] Frame and Label.

  The bars' level animation is Vitals' too: a spring in every bar, kicked when
  something changes the level and left to ring down, and a lean under braking,
  acceleration and landing. BarSlosh and BarLean in [HUD].

0.1.3  --  vending machines and roadside stalls
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE CANDY MACHINES SELL TO YOU NOW, and the soda machines deliberately do not.

  That split is the whole design rather than an oversight. The game already
  sells a drink out of a soda machine, plays its own animation for it and gives
  you a little health -- putting a second, different purchase on top of that is
  two mods fighting over one prop, and it is why machines were left alone in the
  first place. It sells NOTHING out of a candy machine; that one is scenery. So
  the candy machines get a menu and the soda machines are only watched: drink
  from one and the hunger meter notices, which is an observation rather than a
  purchase and cannot collide with anything.

  A machine stocks snacks and drinks, never hot food, and plays the game's own
  vending animation when it sells -- the same one you have seen at the machine
  standing next to it.

  ROADSIDE FRUIT AND VEG STALLS, the same way. The game repeats one stall from
  the Chumash coast to Chiliad -- two of them 2.2km apart under the identical
  canopy -- so they are found by MODEL, exactly as the fridges and the tills
  already are. Every one in the world works, including a map mod's, without a
  single coordinate being written down. What is on the trestle is a named list
  in the ini rather than a category, because "fruit" is not a category: an apple
  is a Snack and a fresh fruit is Food, so filtering by category would either
  miss half the crate or sell beef jerky off it.

  MACHINES AND STALLS ARE ALWAYS CHECKED AFTER THE TILL. A 24/7 has a candy
  machine by the door and a till eight feet behind it, and being handed a bag of
  crisps because you stood slightly to the left of the counter would read as the
  mod picking the wrong thing.

  Four settings under [Counters] turn any of it off, including the sip.

  105 settings, up from 101.

0.1.2  --  the controller, and two things off the comments
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  IT WORKS ON A CONTROLLER NOW, and one part of that was a plain bug. The
  counter and the shelf DREW the pad's own button on their prompt and then read
  the keyboard only, so a controller player was shown a button, pressed it, and
  nothing happened. Sleeping and the fridge already read both; those two had
  been left out. The two panels open on a chord as well -- LB + D-pad Up for the
  settings, RB + X for the pocket -- avoiding LB + D-pad Down, which is Fumes'
  menu, because one chord opening two mods' panels is worse than none.
  Asked for by Jodosodojo.

  DOZING OFF IN A CAR IS A HOLD, not a tap. In a car the interact is the same
  button that orders at a drive-through, so a press meant for lunch put you to
  sleep for four hours instead. A second and a half by default, with a meter on
  screen while it fills; Sleeping/CarHoldSeconds sets it, and 0 puts the tap
  back. Beds are untouched -- nothing competes for the press there, and a hold
  would be friction bought with nothing. Asked for by jerome74.

  A FRIDGE THIS BUILD HAS NOT HEARD OF CAN BE NAMED IN THE INI. Fridges are
  found by model name, so a kitchen from an interior mod -- or a room the game
  added after this was written -- simply had no fridge, and there was nothing
  the player could do but wait for me. Fridge/ExtraModels takes a comma-
  separated list on top of the built-in fourteen. The log line beginning
  "Fridges:" now NAMES the models that did not resolve rather than counting
  them, because a typo and a model your game does not have look identical from
  the kitchen. Reported by jerome74.

  THE SLEEP OFFER IS OURS NOW, not the game's help box. It was pinned to the top
  left in the game's own furniture, next to the messages about your car being
  impounded -- the loudest thing on screen for the quietest prompt in the mod.
  It is a small chip at the bottom middle instead, at two thirds opacity, and
  the hold draws as a thin fill under the words rather than as a row of dots. It
  fades itself out when whatever asked for it stops asking, so it cannot get
  stuck on screen. Three seconds rather than two, now that it is quiet enough to
  afford them.

  101 settings, up from 97 -- and the docs had been saying nine tabs when there
  are six.

0.1.0  --  first release, and a work in progress
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  WHAT IS NOT FINISHED, first, because it is the honest way round: the shop map.
  190 doors are in and they are complete ones, but the convenience stores and
  the fuel stations are thin, and more go in with every update. Everything below
  this line works today.

  HUNGER AND SLEEP, as two thin gauges low on the screen. They are animated and
  they move differently on purpose: hunger churns and gets more agitated the
  emptier it is, sleep breathes and the breath slows as you tire.

  A SECOND HUD IN THE BOX. Switch HUD style to ICONS and the bars come off,
  leaving an apple and a moon that go through five drawings each as they empty
  -- the apple eaten to a core, the eye closing. It reads as a shape rather
  than a length and tells you less on purpose. One press in the F7 menu.

  THEY SIT LOW AND OUT PAST THE RIGHT-HAND EDGE OF THE MAP, and they stay put.
  Both HUDs share the spot, so switching style does not move anything.
  There is an automatic placement that hugs the minimap and it is switched off,
  because it has to ask the game where the map ends and the game answers for a
  STOCK radar -- run anything that has resized yours and the marks land on top
  of it. F7 moves them anywhere you like, live, as you press.

  Both run on the GAME clock, so a day of them is about 48 minutes of play.

  190 PLACES TO BUY FOOD, and they are the real ones: the taco windows, the hot
  dog carts, Burger Shot, Up-n-Atom, Bean Machine, Cool Beans, Rob's Liquor,
  Taco Bomb, Pizza This, thirteen liquor stores, the corner shops, the delis,
  and the restaurants nobody ever gets to walk into. Del Perro and Vespucci are
  covered end to end.

  201 THINGS TO EAT across three categories, each with its own price, its own
  effect and its own line about itself.

  SHOPS WITH A MENU AND STALLS WITH ONE KEY. A counter or a shelf opens a list
  with tabs, pictures and prices. A hot dog cart just hands you a hot dog. The
  drive-throughs work from the driver's seat, and a meal at the wheel is eaten
  slowly between junctions rather than in four seconds at the window.

  A POCKET, on F11. What you buy goes in it by default -- three things -- so a
  shop is somewhere you go back to rather than a place you stand and eat.

  A FRIDGE IN EVERY SAFEHOUSE KITCHEN. Forty slots, your pocket on the left and
  the fridge on the right, one key to move things across and another to eat
  from either side. Found by looking for a fridge rather than by a coordinate,
  so every kitchen the base game put one in works.

  SLEEPING IN ANY BED, with the quality depending on whose bed it is. A car is
  worse than a safehouse and better than nothing. Sleeping sobers you up,
  because it advances the same clock everything else runs on.

  RUNNING COSTS YOU. Sprinting empties both meters faster, and the marks
  visibly speed up while you do it, so the reason is on screen rather than in a
  changelog.

  EMPTY MEANS SOMETHING. Hunger at nothing starts taking health, slowly, with a
  warning first, and stops well above the floor -- it will never kill you.
  Sleep at nothing makes the picture swim, and staying up through that puts you
  on the floor where you stand for four hours.

  DRINK, AND GET DRUNK. Beer, wine and spirits carry their own effect, the walk
  goes with it, and sleeping it off works for free.

  A SETTINGS MENU ON F7. A hundred and five settings across six tabs, each with a
  line saying what it does, everything live as you turn it, and the changes
  written back to the ini without touching your comments or your ordering.

  EVERYTHING IS DATA. The shops, the food, the brands and the social posts are
  four json files in scripts\BareMinimum\. Add a shop, change a price, write a
  menu. Anything the game cannot find is skipped with a line in the log.

  IF YOU ALSO RUN HOODRICH, the shops talk on its feed: 71 accounts and 475
  lines, written as people who sell lunch rather than as gang members. Entirely
  optional, entirely one-way, and completely silent without it.

  Works on GTA V Legacy and Enhanced from the same files. No game file is
  modified and your save is never touched.
